Bottomline Content Agent - Windows x64

1. Extract the ZIP into a folder on your computer.
2. Double-click Bottomline-Content-Agent.exe. No installation is needed.
3. Choose the fictional example business or set up your own business.

Opening can take a minute while Windows unpacks and checks the application.

Your work saves automatically on this Windows account. To update, close the
application and replace the executable with the new version. Your work remains.
Do not run multiple copies while an update is being extracted.

Data and diagnostics: %APPDATA%\Bottomline Content Agent
To back up your work, close the app and copy that entire folder somewhere safe.
Keep backups private: they contain your business information. Windows account
permissions protect these files; the database itself is not encrypted.
You can also export drafts and pilot feedback from inside the app.

Planning, editing, approvals, fictional demo generation and exports work offline.
Real AI generation requires internet and an active Bottomline AI service.
This build has no active AI service unless supplied specifically for your client.
If the header says demo mode, generated samples are templates, not AI output.
No content is automatically published to social media.

If startup fails, close the app and try again. Ensure the ZIP was fully extracted
and that your Windows account can write to its own AppData folder. Contact the
provider with desktop.log from the data folder; do not send your database publicly.

This build is unsigned. Windows may display an unknown publisher or SmartScreen
warning. Obtain a signed client release from the provider if your policy requires it.
Supported target: Windows 10/11 x64. See VERIFICATION.txt for tested coverage.
